Where we started

  • A US triathlon brand selling direct on Shopify
  • $15.6k in gross sales across 2023
  • 164 orders for the whole year

The first goal: double website conversions.

We hit it within a few months. From there the job grew into running marketing end to end: the site, the copy, the product range, email, Meta ads, hiring, and the move into new international markets.

One person across the whole funnel, so every fix pushes in the same direction.

What I worked on

Strategy and hands-on execution, across every channel.

Conversion optimisation

Finding where shoppers dropped off and fixing it page by page, ranked by revenue impact rather than ease.

Copywriting

Headlines, product copy and objection-handling written around why triathletes actually buy.

Product iterations

Shaping the range around what customers were buying, asking for and sending back.

Email marketing

Klaviyo flows and campaigns that turn a first order into the next one.

Meta ads

Creative, campaign structure and scaling, with ads and landing pages built to work together.

Website UI/UX

Design and UX across the store, built for the way shoppers browse on their phones.
