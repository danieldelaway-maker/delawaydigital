Who am I?

The question we all ask ourselves at one point in our lives.

Since you’ve come here to learn about me, whom (I hope) you’ll be working with one day, I’ll save the pitch about why I’m the best guy for the job for the discovery call.

My name is Daniel Delaway but my friends call me Dela. I’m an antique book collector and repairer, soccer player, entrepreneur, and video-biographer. Weird mix I know. I’ve worked on and in businesses both my own and others’ since I was a teenager.

My videography work landed me on national television and my copywriting has made lots of people, lots of money. I love business and building things and helping others reach their full potential. So much so that my friends turned my name into a verb.

To Daniel someone, or Danielling is when I get real with people and cut through a lot of the fluff and excuses we all have a tendency to concoct to delay achieving our dreams, whether personal or business.

In the meantime if you want to talk sci-fi, trivia, history, literature, and business, don’t hesitate to get in touch. But come prepared with at least one fun fact.

Oh and that’s a photo with me and my grandmother after I interviewed her about her life story. I’m a video-biographer on the side and have interviewed over 100 people from Holocaust Survivors to Sex Workers and everything in between.

Let’s have a chat and see if we’re a good fit to work together.
