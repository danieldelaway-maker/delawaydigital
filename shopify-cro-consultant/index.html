Most Shopify stores don’t have a traffic problem. They have a conversion problem.

Every extra visitor you buy costs more than the last. Conversion rate optimisation (CRO) is how you get more revenue out of the traffic you already have: fixing the confusion, friction and missing reasons-to-buy that quietly send people back to Google.

Shopify CRO FAQ

What does a Shopify CRO consultant do?

A Shopify CRO consultant finds the reasons visitors leave without buying and fixes them. That means auditing your store and analytics, improving product pages, copy, cart and checkout, and measuring the impact on conversion rate and revenue.

How quickly will I see results?

Quick wins like clearer product copy, stronger trust signals and checkout fixes often go live within the first few weeks. Bigger gains from testing and ongoing optimisation build over the following months.

Do I need a lot of traffic for CRO?

No. A/B testing needs enough traffic to reach a reliable result, but most stores gain far more from research-led fixes to obvious friction points first. I’ll recommend the right approach for your traffic level.

Do you implement the changes or just advise?

Either. I can hand over a prioritised audit for your team, or rewrite, redesign and rebuild the pages myself inside Shopify.

What’s a good conversion rate for a Shopify store?

It varies a lot by industry, price point and traffic source, so beware of generic benchmarks. The numbers that matter most are your own trend over time and your revenue per visitor.

How much does Shopify CRO cost?

It depends on scope: a one-off audit costs far less than ongoing optimisation. Book a free site walkthrough and I’ll recommend the right option for your store before you spend anything.
