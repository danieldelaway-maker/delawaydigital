FAQ

What happens on the free site walkthrough?

We jump on a video call and I go through your homepage and a key product page with you, live. I’ll point out the biggest conversion leaks as we go and how I’d fix them, so you leave with a shortlist you can act on yourself. No obligation to work together.

How quickly will I see results?

Quick wins like copy, page fixes and email flows usually go live in the first few weeks. Bigger gains from testing, ads and strategy build over the following months.

What’s the difference between CRO and a Fractional CMO?

CRO focuses on getting more of your existing visitors to buy. As a Fractional CMO I look after the whole marketing engine (ads, email, copy, launches and strategy) part-time, for a fraction of the cost of a full-time hire.

Do you only work with Shopify stores?

Most of my e-commerce clients are on Shopify, but I’ve also worked with SaaS, fintech, service businesses and Web3 launches. The principles of why people buy don’t change.

How involved do I need to be?

After a kickoff call and access to your store, analytics and ad accounts, I take the lead. You’ll get regular updates and a quick check-in so you always know what’s happening and why.

What if it doesn’t work?

Every recommendation is tied to a number we agree on up front, so you’ll always know whether it’s working. And the free walkthrough costs you nothing, so you can see how I think before spending a cent.
