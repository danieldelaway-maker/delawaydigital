  • You’re doing real revenue, but marketing still falls to the founder at night

A fractional CMO gives you senior marketing leadership for a fraction of the cost of a full-time hire.

Fractional CMO FAQ

What is a fractional CMO?

A fractional CMO is a senior marketing leader who works with your business part-time. You get the strategy, leadership and experience of a Chief Marketing Officer without the cost or commitment of a full-time hire.

How is a fractional CMO different from an agency?

An agency usually executes one channel, like ads or SEO. A fractional CMO owns the overall strategy, connects every channel and manages any agencies or freelancers you already have. I also do much of the hands-on work myself.

How much time do you spend with each client?

It depends on your stage and goals. We agree a set scope on our first call, and it can scale up for launches and back down once things are running smoothly.

How much does a fractional CMO cost?

Far less than a full-time CMO, because you only pay for the time and scope you need. Book a free call and I’ll recommend a setup that fits your budget and goals.

Do you work with brands outside Australia?

Yes. I’m based in Melbourne and work with e-commerce brands in Australia, the US, Europe and beyond, including US and EU product launches.

How quickly can you start?

Usually within a week or two. The first step is a free call to understand your business, followed by a deep-dive into your marketing and data.
